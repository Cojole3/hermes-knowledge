# Lacande design language

The main rules of how Lacande looks and sounds. The source of truth is the website's guidelines.
Updated: 2026-10-20

**Source of truth:** `docs/GUIDELINES.md` and `docs/for Leon/lacande-positioning.md` in the Lacande-Website repository. If this page disagrees with them, they win.

## Positioning
- **Lacande is a maison d'édition** ("it edits things that have no body"). It acts as the publisher: it commissions and signs work it did not make itself.
- **Doctrine:** "Modernisme humaniste".
- **References:** ECM, Blue Note, Gallimard NRF. Provence is a recurring theme.
- **Products are "numbered like a record and credited like a book":**
  - **LC-** numbers are candles.
  - **AS-** numbers are matchbox éditions.
  - **CP-** numbers are curated playlists. A *hors-série* is a playlist without a candle.

## Visual rules
- **Restraint:** editorial and calm. The reference is lido-lido.com.
- **Type:** titles in **Spectral**, everything else in **Mattone**. Each product has its own display typeface.
- **Colour:** one house red, **Select red #C50141**. Music uses **Ultramarine**.
- **Print references:** riso printing and Olivetti graphics.
- **Scent notes:** never shown as a top/heart/base pyramid.

## Tagline on the label
"Light it. · Tap the label. · Listen."
