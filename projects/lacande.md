# Lacande

Leon's main business: a small French house making "listening candles", where each candle pairs a scent with a playlist.
Updated: 2026-10-20

## What it is
**The idea:** each candle is a perfume and a music playlist in one object. An NFC tag in the label opens the playlist: "Light it. · Tap the label. · Listen."

**Positioning:** a *maison d'édition* that commissions and signs work. See [../design/lacande-design-language.md](../design/lacande-design-language.md).

**Team:** Leon (founder, product owner, direction) and one co-founder. The perfumes are by Jean-Michel Duriez.

## Products
- **Candles:** 250 g each, about 60 h burn time. The €85 price is a placeholder.
  - LC-001 Jazz Impressions (formerly "Tournant Jazz")
  - LC-002 Sonata in Venetian Rose
  - LC-003 Isola Paradiso
  - LC-004 Nuit d'Amour
- **Matchbox éditions:** numbered, e.g. AS-001 Arles. The first real print job is matchbox model BX-L50.
- **Playlists:** numbered CP-; the latest is CP-007 Calypso Blues. A *hors-série* is a playlist without a candle.
- **Journal:** editorial writing on the website.
