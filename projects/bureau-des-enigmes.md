# Le Bureau des Énigmes (folder "Settimana enigmistica")

A French cultural puzzle magazine (games, cooking and editorial under one membership), built as a mobile-first web app.
Updated: 2026-10-20

## What it is
- **Audience:** urban, culturally engaged French readers aged 25–42. The pitch is "NYT Games × La Settimana Enigmistica × Les Inrocks". Digital first, print later.
- **Content:** produced by an LLM pipeline, with light human review.
- **Games:** Mot Perdu (Wordle-style), Cousinades (Connections-style), Trivia, Sudoku, La Tour Épelle (with a co-op "équipe" mode) and Vieux Jeu.
- **Also built:**
  - a membership club ("Le Club", tiers from Enquêteuse to Détective);
  - badges;
  - cookie-free analytics;
  - a "Studio" back office.

## How it's built
- **Code:** Next.js 16, React, TypeScript, Supabase and Tailwind v4, in a pnpm/Turborepo monorepo (`apps/web`, `apps/studio`).
- **Hosting:** Vercel.
- **Daily puzzles:** generated by GitHub Actions.
